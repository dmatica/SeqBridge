SeqBridge Support

Help, troubleshooting and contact for SeqBridge — a macOS app for browsing and transferring data in Illumina BaseSpace Sequence Hub.

Getting started

  1. Grant the folder. On first launch SeqBridge explains, then asks for access to a folder named .basespace in your home folder, creating it if it is not there. This is the same folder the official BaseSpace CLI uses, so profiles you already have are picked up automatically.
  2. Sign in. Click Sign In with BaseSpace, pick your region, and name the profile.
  3. Approve in the browser. SeqBridge shows a short code and opens Illumina's sign-in page. Approve there and the profile is ready. Nothing else needs to be installed.

Common questions

Why is SeqBridge asking for access to a folder?
SeqBridge is sandboxed, so it cannot read anything on your Mac until you say it may. It asks once for .basespace, where connection profiles live, and after that for nothing except the folders you pick for downloads. It cannot read anywhere else.
The folder prompt appeared and I dismissed it. How do I get it back?
The main window offers Grant Access…, and clicking Sign In with BaseSpace will ask again as well. Nothing is lost by declining the first time.
My region is missing from the sign-in picker.
SeqBridge is registered in every BaseSpace region except Middle East. Unregistered regions are shown but disabled, with the reason, rather than failing after you have already approved in a browser. If you need Middle East, please email — knowing there is demand is what moves it up the list.
Sign-in opened my browser but the app never picked it up.
The approval and the app have to reach the same BaseSpace region. Check the region in the sign-in sheet matches the account you approved with, and that no VPN or proxy is blocking the app's own connection. If it still hangs, send the log (below).
A download or upload failed partway through.
Transfers survive individual files failing and report which ones did. Downloads can be cancelled and leave nothing behind. Files over 5 GiB upload in parts. If a transfer fails repeatedly, the log names the file and the error.
Where are my logs, and what is in them?
In the app: Help → Open Log Folder… (⇧⌘L). Logs stay on your Mac and are never transmitted. Attaching the relevant one makes a transfer problem far easier to diagnose — do glance through first for anything you would rather not send.
What does SeqBridge collect about me?
Nothing. There is no analytics and no telemetry, and no server run by the developer: the app talks only to the BaseSpace instance you sign in to. See the privacy policy.
How do I remove SeqBridge completely?
Drag the app to the Trash. Connection profiles in ~/.basespace are shared with the BaseSpace CLI and are left alone — delete that folder yourself if you want them gone. Nothing of yours is stored anywhere else.

Reporting a problem

Whatever route you use, these four things get to an answer fastest:

  • What you were doing, and what happened instead
  • Your macOS version and whether the Mac is Apple Silicon or Intel
  • The BaseSpace region the profile uses
  • The log from Help → Open Log Folder…, if it is a transfer problem
