# SeqBridge Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 10, 2026

## Data Collection

SeqBridge does not collect, store, or transmit any user data.

## What SeqBridge Does

- Connects directly to your Illumina BaseSpace account
- All data transfers occur directly between your computer and BaseSpace servers
- No data passes through our servers
- No analytics or tracking is performed
- No user information is collected

## Third-Party Services

SeqBridge connects to:
- **Illumina BaseSpace Sequence Hub**: Your data is subject to Illumina's privacy policy at https://www.illumina.com/company/legal/privacy.html

## Performance Monitoring

SeqBridge uses Apple's MetricKit framework for anonymous performance monitoring:
- Metrics are aggregated and anonymized by Apple before we see them
- No user identification or personal data is included
- This can be disabled in macOS System Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements

## Your BaseSpace Data

- Your BaseSpace credentials are stored securely as set by the BaseSpace Command-Line Interface 
- We never see or store your credentials
- All BaseSpace data remains under your control
